⚡ Instant Height Limitation Vetting Workflow

Applicant inputs Latitude, Longitude, Ground Level (GL), and Proposed Height. The system combines digital elevation models (SRTM/LiDAR) with 3D CAD 14 surfaces to deliver an immediate Approved Height Clearance Certificate or Prescribed Mitigation Notice.

⚖️ Automated Section 24O(2) Statutory Enforcement Engine

When an AOL failure occurs in an active flight approach path:
1. System triggers an automated ATC NOTAM Warning within 60 seconds.
2. Automated SMS/Email warning dispatched to tower owner (Maxis/edotco/TNB).
3. If unrectified within statutory deadline, system automatically generates a Section 24O(2) Compound Notice.

Program Pemutihan — National Structure Legalization

👤 Suria Ramadhan for En. Zarwavai Suratman (C Edge Sdn Bhd)

GREENFIELD & BROWNFIELD LEGALIZATION PIPELINE
Transforming Unrecorded Liabilities into Compliant Assets

Phased national campaign empowering telcos, power companies, and developers to regularize assets with CAAM.

🏢 Greenfield (New) vs Brownfield (Existing)

Two-Track Onboarding
Greenfield Application:
Mandatory height clearance approval prior to local council (PBT) development order issuance.
Brownfield Legalization:
Amnesty registration of existing unrecorded towers with retrofitted certified AOL lights.

📦 Bulk Enterprise Ingestion Tools

Major infrastructure providers (edotco, EdgePoint, DNB, Tenaga Nasional Berhad) can batch-upload thousands of tower coordinates via GeoJSON/KML or REST API, reducing onboarding time from months to hours.
