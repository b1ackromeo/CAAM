Six Critical Regulatory Vulnerabilities

👤 Suria Ramadhan for En. Zarwavai Suratman (C Edge Sdn Bhd)
Operational fragmentation across CAAM divisions leads to severe compliance risks, revenue leakage, and public safety exposure.
🏗️ Act 771 S.24O Violation

1. Unmonitored Obstacle Lights

Over 14,200 telecommunication and transmission structures nationwide lack live telemetry. Following the July 2022 OLC revocation, CAAM officers have zero real-time visibility over unlit hazard towers in active flight approach funnels.

⚠️ Risk: Aircraft collision hazard · RM 100k fine uncollected
🪪 ICAO Annex 1 Audit Lag

2. Paper Crew Verification

Manual 30-day licensing cycle requiring physical counter visits in Putrajaya. Foreign civil aviation authorities must wait 5–10 business days for email confirmations to verify Malaysian pilot credentials due to the lack of an immutable public verification ledger.

⚠️ Risk: Licensing backlog · Foreign credential delays
🛸 MCAR 2016 Reg 140

3. Unregulated UTM Airspace

Commercial drone operators endure 14-day manual email processing for Authorization to Fly (ATF) permits. Air traffic controllers have zero real-time radar or Remote ID tracking for unauthorized drone intrusions within 5km airport Control Zones (CTR).

⚠️ Risk: Near-miss collisions · 14-day commercial bottleneck
🔧 ICAO Annex 6 & 8

4. Disconnected Airworthiness

AOC operator maintenance logs and MOR mandatory incident reports are siloed across disparate spreadsheets. Safety inspections occur reactively on fixed calendars rather than predictively driven by machine learning anomaly detection.

⚠️ Risk: Undetected mechanical faults · Reactive safety posture
👥 MACPC 2016 Mandate

5. Post-MAVCOM Portal Gap

Following the 1 August 2025 merger, CAAM lacks a unified digital consumer portal for passenger flight rights, luggage compensation tracking, airline on-time SLA ranking, and commercial Air Service Licence (ASL) economic oversight.

⚠️ Risk: Passenger complaint backlogs · Regulatory blindspot
💰 Revenue Leakage

6. RM 28M Uncollected Fees

Unregistered obstacle towers, unmonitored commercial drone operations, delayed AOC renewal fees, and unautomated statutory fine collection cause over RM 28 Million in lost annual regulatory revenues that should fund national aviation infrastructure.

⚠️ Risk: Fiscal burden on Treasury · Zero self-funding model
