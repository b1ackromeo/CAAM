# Proposed Digital Solutions for Civil Aviation Authority of Malaysia (CAAM)

Based on a comprehensive review of CAAM's expanded roles—particularly its integration with the Malaysian Aviation Commission (MAVCOM) in August 2025—CAAM is now responsible for both the **technical safety** and **economic regulation** of Malaysia's aviation sector. 

Setting aside the Height Limitation/AOL system, here are four high-impact web application systems that can be proposed to modernize CAAM's core functions:

---

## 1. National UAS/Drone Traffic Management System (UAS-TMS)
**Target Division:** Flight Operations (Unmanned Aircraft Systems)

**The Problem:** Commercial and recreational drone usage is exploding. Currently, applying for an Authorisation to Fly (ATF) is a manual process that takes up to 14 days, making it difficult to enforce regulations, track illegal flights, or manage airspace safely.

**The Proposed System:**
A centralized, real-time portal for all drone-related activities.
- **Features:**
  - **E-Registration & Licensing:** Digital registration for drones and licensing for remote pilots (Remote Pilot Certificate).
  - **Automated ATF Approvals:** Quick approvals for low-risk flights based on predefined parameters.
  - **Interactive Geofencing Map:** A live map showing restricted areas (airports, military bases, prisons) where drone flights are blocked.
  - **Flight Tracking:** Integration with drone APIs to monitor active commercial flights in real-time, aiding enforcement by CAAM and the Royal Malaysia Police (PDRM).

## 2. Integrated Aviation Personnel e-Licensing Portal
**Target Division:** Personnel Licensing

**The Problem:** Managing the licenses of pilots, air traffic controllers, and aircraft maintenance engineers (AMEs) involves heavy paperwork, manual logbook verifications, and physical medical certificates.

**The Proposed System:**
A secure, unified digital identity and licensing platform for aviation professionals.
- **Features:**
  - **Digital Logbooks:** Pilots and engineers can log flight hours and maintenance tasks digitally, verified by their employers.
  - **Automated Renewals:** Alerts for expiring licenses and automated renewal workflows once required hours and training are verified.
  - **Medical Examiner Portal:** Designated Aviation Medical Examiners (DAMEs) can directly upload medical clearance certificates to a pilot's profile.
  - **E-Exams Integration:** Booking and tracking of theoretical and practical examination results.

## 3. Aviation Consumer Protection & Dispute Resolution Platform
**Target Division:** Economic and Commercial Regulation (Former MAVCOM function)

**The Problem:** With the absorption of MAVCOM, CAAM is now responsible for protecting consumer rights. Passengers facing flight delays, cancellations, or lost baggage need a transparent way to seek compensation from airlines.

**The Proposed System:**
A public-facing consumer rights portal to mediate disputes between passengers and airlines.
- **Features:**
  - **E-Filing for Complaints:** Passengers can easily file claims against airlines, uploading tickets, boarding passes, and receipts.
  - **Automated SLA Tracking:** The system enforces the Malaysian Aviation Consumer Protection Code (MACPC) by setting strict countdown timers (e.g., 30 days) for airlines to resolve cases.
  - **Airline Compliance Dashboard:** CAAM administrators can view which airlines have the highest complaint rates and whether they are violating resolution timelines, allowing CAAM to issue automated penalties.

## 4. Digital Airworthiness & Fleet Tracking System
**Target Division:** Airworthiness

**The Problem:** Ensuring that every registered commercial and private aircraft in Malaysia is safe to fly requires tracking millions of maintenance records, Airworthiness Directives (ADs), and service bulletins.

**The Proposed System:**
A compliance and tracking system for Approved Maintenance Organizations (AMOs) and airlines.
- **Features:**
  - **E-Certificates of Airworthiness (CoA):** Digital issuance and renewal of aircraft CoAs and Certificates of Registration.
  - **AD/SB Broadcast System:** CAAM can instantly push mandatory Airworthiness Directives to all affected aircraft owners and track their compliance status in real-time.
  - **Audit & Inspection App:** A tablet-friendly module for CAAM inspectors to log findings, upload photos, and issue digital rectification notices during ramp inspections or hangar audits.
